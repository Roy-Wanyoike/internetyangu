Investor Concept Report
LinkPulse
Every connection. Every shilling. One dashboard.
A universal connectivity and spend monitor for East Africa, generalizing the open-source Dishylink pattern from Starlink to every network a household or small business touches: mobile data, home fiber, public WiFi and satellite. LinkPulse shows what you are connected to, how it actually performs, and what it really costs — then acts on your behalf.
