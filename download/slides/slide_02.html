THE PROBLEM

Connectivity is a portfolio.
Nobody is keeping score.

A typical East African household juggles Safaricom bundles, home fiber from Zuku, Faiba, Airtel or Poa, public WiFi and mobile money receipts - each with its own price per GB, expiry clock and failure mode. The information lives in transient SMS, USSD menus and rival carrier apps.
